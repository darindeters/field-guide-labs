# Review instructions

## What Important means here
Important: wrong totals, wrong discounts, or a unit change a caller misses.
Style and naming are Nit at most.

## Cap the nits
Report at most three Nits per review.

## Do not report
- Generated files under `billing/gen/`
- Anything CI already enforces: lint, formatting, types

## Evidence
Behavior claims need a file:line citation, not an inference from naming.
